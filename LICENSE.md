## 🛠️ Troubleshooting Common Issues

---

❌ **GUI doesn't appear after executing the script**

The script loaded but nothing is visible on screen after injection.

**Solution:** Your executor may not fully support the GUI renderer. Update your executor to the latest version, re-inject the script, and wait 3–4 seconds. If it still doesn't appear, try toggling fullscreen mode off in Roblox settings.

---

❌ **INF Money not applying to wallet balance**

The toggle is on but in-game money isn't increasing.

**Solution:** This feature requires a brief in-game trigger — complete one delivery run or pick up one loot crate after enabling it. The hook activates on the next legitimate money event. It won't retroactively fill the wallet on a completely cold start.

---

❌ **Script crashes immediately after injection on Android**

Executor crashes back to the home screen right after execution.

**Solution:** Your Android executor may be running an outdated injection method. Make sure your executor app has been updated within the last 30 days. Also check that the `southernmudding.lua` file wasn't corrupted during download — re-download it and try again.

---

❌ **iOS certificate is revoked and executor won't open**

The sideloaded executor app shows a "not trusted" error after an iOS update.

**Solution:** Go to **Settings → General → VPN & Device Management**, find the developer certificate, and tap **Trust**. If the certificate has been fully revoked by Apple, you'll need to re-sideload the `.ipa` file using your preferred sideloading method.

---

❌ **Auto Farm stops after a few minutes**

The auto farm feature runs for a while but then goes idle.

**Solution:** This usually happens when the script loses its server connection reference after a Roblox micro-disconnect. Re-toggle the Auto Farm switch off and back on to restart the pathing loop. Enabling Anti-Kick in the config helps prevent the underlying disconnects.

---

❌ **Speed Hack making the vehicle teleport instead of moving smoothly**

Vehicle jerks or teleports across the map instead of accelerating smoothly.

**Solution:** Your `fly_speed` or `speed_multiplier` config values are set too high. Open `config.json` at `C:/Users/YourName/AppData/Local/SMScript/config.json` and reduce `speed_multiplier` to `1.5` or lower. Values above `3.0` are known to cause physics desync on the server side.
